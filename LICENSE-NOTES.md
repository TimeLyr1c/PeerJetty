# Source and license status

`Original/` preserves the supplied OpenOnMini-Starter source and accompanying documents as a local development baseline. `Assets/AppIcon.png` and `Scripts/make_icns.pl` currently originate from that baseline. The starter did not contain an explicit license. Preservation and local adaptation do not establish permission for public redistribution.

Before publishing on GitHub, obtain the starter author's explicit source/icon license or replace the borrowed implementation/assets and omit the Original baseline from the public repository. Then choose and add a license for the final project, with any required attribution. No permissive license is asserted here on behalf of the original author.

The new source does not embed device-specific connection information or keys. Runtime Keychain identities, local configuration, transfer data and build caches are excluded from Git. Development documents and screenshots must also be reviewed before publishing.
